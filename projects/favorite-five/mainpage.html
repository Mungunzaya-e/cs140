Why Christmas?

Well, since Halloween is over, I think it's already time for Christmas to shine. It's my favorite holiday, and favorite time of the year. I love the warm spirit it brings into the whole world and the whole world unites to celebrate the birth of the Savior either consciously or subconsciously.

Christ

First, the whole reason we are here celebrating this holiday is our Savior Jesus Christ. I'm beyond grateful for everything He has chosen to do for us.

Lights

I just cannot imagine Christmas without all kinds of decorative lights. Also, light is a direct symbol of Christ.

Music

I love Christmas music. It just instantly brings the Christmas Spirit whenever you listen to them.

Gifts

Gifts are part of the important symbols of Christmas. Because He gave us the greatest gift of life, we too can spread His love through gift giving.

Christmas Tree

Of course, we cannot imagine Christmas without Christmas Trees as well. I love that it symbolizes Christ's resurrection and eternal life.

Hot Chocolate

Lastly, a cup of hot chocolate to keep you warm, specially if you are in Mongolia.
